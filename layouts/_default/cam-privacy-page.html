1. Information HandyCam Accesses and Why

If you grant HandyCam permission, it accesses the following on your device. Each is used only for the purpose described, and none of it is sent off your device by HandyCam.

  • Camera
    Purpose: This lets you see a live preview and take photos and videos with manual controls. Camera images are processed on your device only, including the live histogram, focus peaking, long exposure and RAW features.
  • Microphone
    Purpose: This lets you record sound with your videos. HandyCam asks for it only when you switch to video mode.
  • Location
    Purpose: This is optional and off by default. If you turn on Geotag in the options menu and allow it, HandyCam adds your approximate location to the photos and videos you take, so they show where they were taken. The location is read only while the app is open, is stored only inside those photos and videos in your Photos library, and is never sent anywhere by HandyCam.
  • Photos
    Purpose: This lets you save the photos and videos you take to your photo library, and lets HandyCam show your latest photo and the library browser inside the app. If you choose limited access, HandyCam sees only the photos you select.

HandyCam also uses the following without needing a permission:

  • Motion sensors
    Purpose: The level overlay reads your device's motion sensors to show whether it is level. The readings are used live on your device and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Settings stored on your device
    Purpose: HandyCam remembers your last settings (mode, ISO, shutter speed, aperture, white balance, focus and exposure compensation) and any presets you save, including the names you give them. These stay in the app's storage on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

HandyCam asks for your location only if you turn on Geotag, and adds it to your photos only then. Photos and videos you save carry the usual camera information, such as the time they were taken and the exposure settings.

Please note that you can decline a permission or revoke it after you granted it in the iOS settings. If you do so, HandyCam cannot use that feature: without camera access it cannot show a preview, without microphone access videos have no sound, and without Photos access it cannot save or show your photos.

HandyCam has no accounts and no network features, so we do not receive any information about you or your device from the app. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous crash and usage statistics. We cannot identify you from them.

2. Personal Information Sharing

HandyCam does not share your personal information with anyone. It does not include third-party services, analytics, advertising or social media features.

Photos and videos you save are stored in your Apple Photos library. If you use features such as iCloud Photos, they are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by HandyCam. If you share a photo or video from the Photos app, that is your own action.

3. Tracking Technologies

HandyCam does not track you. It does not use advertising identifiers or device identifiers, and it does not include any tracking technologies from third parties. Because there is no tracking, there is nothing to opt out of.

4. Privacy Rights of California Residents

You can also authorize an agent to exercise rights on your behalf. If you have any questions or would like to exercise any of your rights, please contact us at the contact information below.

Collection, Sale and Disclosure of Personal Information

In the preceding 12 months we have not collected any personal information through HandyCam, we have not sold any personal information, and we have not disclosed any personal information for business purposes. We do not sell the personal information of minors under 16 years of age.

Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights:

  • Right to know: Twice in a 12-month period, request a copy of your personal information that we collected, used, disclosed, and sold, if any
  • Right to delete: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to opt out: Opt out from the sale, if any, of your personal information

Because HandyCam keeps no personal information about you on our systems, we would have nothing to disclose or delete. You can exercise your privacy rights generally for free. To verify a request, please contact us at the contact information below.

HandyCam processes your photos, videos and settings only on your device and we hold no personal information about you.

6. Children's Personal Information

HandyCam does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and it has no accounts or online features. If you become aware that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at the contact information below.

7. How Long We Keep Your Information

HandyCam stores nothing on servers. Your settings and presets stay on your device until you change them or delete the app. Photos and videos you save stay in your Photos library until you delete them.

8. How We Protect Your Information

HandyCam does not transmit your data, so there is no server to protect. Your photos and videos are kept in your Apple Photos library and are protected by the security features of your device, such as your passcode and iOS data protection. Temporary files that HandyCam creates while recording video or saving RAW photos are removed once the item has been saved.

9. Policy Changes

When we make material changes to this privacy policy, for example, due to app updates or changes in the law, we will post the updated policy here. The policy will always apply in the version at the time of your use of HandyCam, even if you downloaded HandyCam at a time when a previous policy version was applicable.
